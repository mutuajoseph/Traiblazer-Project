Busting Campus Myths

Common dismissive labels in secondary schools unpacked with real psychological truth:

“Anajifanya tu mgonjwa” ⌄

Psychosomatic stomach pains and sudden tension headaches before exams are real nervous system responses, not drama or deceit.

“Omba tu, ni pepo ya uvivu” ⌄

Faith is comforting, but depression and anxiety are health conditions that deserve care, rest, and qualified support.

“Huyu anataka attention tu” ⌄

When someone reaches out, it can signal a need for safety, validation, or support. They deserve to be heard.

Being sent 300km away from home to an Extra-County boarding school made me cry for three straight weeks as a Form 1. I felt like an alien.
